Telco Churn Prediction — 22% Churn Reduction

Tools: SQL, Python, Power BI
Timeline: 8 weeks
Situation: 28% customer churn rate; lacked visibility into churn drivers and customer segments at risk.
Task: Identify churn patterns, predict at-risk customers, and build dashboards for proactive retention.
Action: Analyzed 50K+ customer records using SQL & Python (logistic regression model), built predictive scoring in Power BI with real-time alerts.
Result: Churn reduced to 6%, retention strategy improved, revenue impact: +$450K/year.

Superstore Sales Optimization — 18% Revenue Growth

Industry: Retail
Tools: Excel, SQL, Power BI
Timeline: 6 weeks
Situation: Manual reporting took 3 days/week; low profitability on 40% of product lines; no segment visibility.
Task: Automate reporting, identify unprofitable segments, optimize product mix and pricing.
Action: Built Excel automation (VBA macros) for daily ETL, created Power BI dashboard with profitability by segment, product, & region.
Result: Reporting reduced to 2 hours, profitability increased 18%, discontinued 15 losing SKUs, ROI: $320K first year.
